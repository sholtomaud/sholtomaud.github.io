Relocated as a boy while his father fought in an Imperial fleet conflict, the privileged son of an Imperial Navy Captain becomes a luxury deathsticks salesman under a brutal local Apartheid regime—a regime the Empire secretly plots to destabilize. But after a corrupt acquittal for a native laborer’s death sparks a tragic family loss, he orchestrates a dangerous campaign of sabotage that traps him inside the ISB as a deep-cover double agent.
