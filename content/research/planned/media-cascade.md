This project aims to automate a technique known as 'Media Cascade' developed by Edelman et al. (2011). The automation is explored by using the image capabilities of Large Language Models. The goal is to then further extend the Media Cascade with Dynamic Systems Modelling. Can an LLM identify systems and components depicted in an image, and then deconstruct the system into Ordinary Differential Equations for computational simulation purposes?

[Edelman, J., Currano, R., 2011. Re-representation: Affordances of Shared Models in Team-Based Design, in: Meinel, C., Leifer, L., Plattner, H., Design Thinking: Understand – Improve – Apply. Springer, Berlin, Heidelberg, pp. 61–79.](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-13757-0_4)
